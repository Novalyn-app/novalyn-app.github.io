Questions, answered

Is Novalyn made by Vinted?

No. Novalyn is an independent tool for sellers and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Vinted.

How does a “relist” actually work?

Vinted has no one-tap relist, so Novalyn does what you'd do by hand: it creates a copy of your listing (photos and details included), confirms it is live, then deletes the original — saving a full backup first so you can always restore it.

Do you collect my data?

No. There is no account and nothing about you or your listings is sent to us. The licence key is verified on your own device, and your listing data never leaves your browser. See the privacy policy.

Where do I get my licence key?

It's emailed to you automatically, usually within a minute or two of payment. If you don't see it, check your spam folder — that's where it most often ends up. Paste it into the extension's licence card — the first thing on the Records page — and you're done. No login.

My plan renews — do I need to do anything?

Yes, one thing. Every time a monthly or yearly plan renews, we email you a new key: paste it into the same field to keep relisting unlimited. When the previous key reaches its expiry date it stops working, and relisting pauses until the new one is pasted in (if the renewal email hasn't arrived, check your spam folder). A Lifetime licence never expires, so there's nothing to do later.

What does “Lifetime” cover exactly?

A Lifetime licence is one payment: the key never expires, there are no renewals, and you keep unlimited relisting for as long as Novalyn is offered and keeps working with Vinted. We can't promise that for ever, though — Vinted can change its site or its rules in a way no relisting tool can follow. If that ever happened, the extension would stop being able to relist, and this is the limit of what any one-time purchase can cover. See our terms.

What happens after my 50 free relists?

Relisting pauses until you add a licence key. Your listings, records, history and archived backups stay fully available — you just can't start a new relist.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. See the refund policy for the window and how to ask.
