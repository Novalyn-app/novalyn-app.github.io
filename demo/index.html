What to try

  • Listings — tick two items and press Relist. Nothing happens on purpose: a demo that pretended to change your wardrobe would be a lie.
  • Archive — the backup of every original, with a restore button. This is what makes a failed relist recoverable.
  • Results — views per day before and after a relist, and the ads that are not improving.
  • Automation — a prepared run waiting for approval, which is the default for a reason.
  • Settings — including the dark appearance for Vinted's own pages.
