Licence

—

A licence unlocks unlimited relists. Keep it somewhere safe: you will need to paste it again after reinstalling the extension or moving to another computer.

How to activate
  1. Choose a plan below and pay in the Stripe tab that opens. Your licence key is emailed automatically to the address you fill in at checkout — usually within a minute or two. If it has not arrived, check your spam folder: it often ends up there.
  2. Paste the key into the field above — it starts with NV1. or NV2. — and click Activate.
  3. That is it: relisting is unlimited from then on. The key works on every computer you use; a monthly or yearly key runs to its renewal date — and at each renewal we email you a fresh key to paste in here.

A Lifetime licence is a one-time payment: the key never expires and there are no renewals. It gives you unlimited relists for as long as Novalyn is offered and keeps working with Vinted. We cannot promise that forever, because Vinted can change its site or its rules in ways no relisting tool can follow.
